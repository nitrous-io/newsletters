Nitrous.IO Now Supports WebSockets

This month we've added much needed support for WebSockets. WebSockets allow bi-directional communication over HTTP between the client and server. This allows you to create multiplayer games, chat rooms, and other collaborative apps.

Head over to Nitrous.IO and try it out. Take a look at our support guide to help get started.


Meteor Devshop 8

Recently we visited Meteor Devshop 8 to present the new Autoparts package manager, which allows you to setup Meteor in a single command. Read the Meteor article for a recap of the Nitrous.IO talk, as well as other talks from the same evening.

Looking to build an app with Meteor? Take a look at Discover Meteor's guide on building a Meteor App on Nitrous.IO.
