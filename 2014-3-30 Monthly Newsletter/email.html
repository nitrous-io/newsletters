Nitrous.IO raises $6.65M Series A financing from Bessemer Venture Partners

This month, Nitrous.IO closed a $6.65M Series A financing with Bessemer Venture Partners.

Our mission with Nitrous.IO is to improve the way you work and collaborate on software applications by making development environments easier to create, configure and share.

With this funding, we can aggressively pursue that mission with the best people working to perfect the experience for you, the developer. We want to empower you to build amazing applications in less time, with less frustration. You can also read more about the funding announcement on our blog.

We appreciate all of the valuable feedback and support, we’re incredibly lucky to be building a service for developers like you -- here’s to the future of development environments!


Custom Domain Support Now Available

This month we are excited to announce that you can now use custom domains with applications on your Nitrous.IO box.

Live preview is an awesome feature that let's you easily share what you're working on for rapid feedback and iteration. By default your app is "preview-ed" at URL that is based on the name of your development box.

To get started, sign up for a Shift of Turbo plan and follow our help guide to get setup.

Stability and a Linux OOM killer bug

The Nitrous.IO team has been dedicated to improving system stabability and user experience. Recently, we deployed a kernel patch which resolved a stability issue that affected some customers.

Read our blog post for a full story on how our dev team brought stability back to our high expectation levels.

Node.JS, Python, Go, and Other Language Support in Autoparts

As more packages are added to Autoparts, setting up your development environment is easier than ever. With Autoparts you can now install Node.JS, Python, Go, and many other languages on a single box.

Other parts which have been added include Erlang, Haskell, Google AppEngine, Cloud Foundry, Zsh, Tcsh, Fish, PHPUnit, PHPMyAdmin, Beanstalk, and more.

If you want to contribute to the Autoparts package manager, take a look at the Contributing to Autoparts guide for full documentation.
