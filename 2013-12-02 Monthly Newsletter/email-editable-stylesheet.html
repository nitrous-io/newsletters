Pro Tip: Keep Sessions Alive with Tmux

Have you need in the situation where you wanted to share your Preview URL with a friend, only to see that the app stops running once you close your browser? Have you lost a connection at some point and wish you could resume your work as it was within the terminal?

Every Nitrous.IO box contains the tool Tmux, which allows you to create a session which can be joined at a later period of time. By using Tmux you will also be able to keep your app running for an extended period of time after you disconnect from your box.
