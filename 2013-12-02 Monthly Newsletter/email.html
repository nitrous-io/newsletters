Stop fighting with Production Environments

One of the challenges that we often hear from developers is the difficulty in managing the states of the various development and production environments they need to use on a regular basis.

Nitrous.IO solves the problem with simple, repeatable configurations for development that can transition easily into to the production environment. Take a look at our blog post about Cloud 66, and how you can put your app into a production environment within 60 datacenters globally.

Pro Tip: Keep Sessions Alive with Tmux

If you have ran into the situation where you want to share your preview URL after you have disconnected from your Nitrous box, or just want to save a console session to resume from at a later time, take a look at Tmux.

Every Nitrous.IO box contains the tool Tmux, which allows you to create a session which can be joined at a later period of time. By using Tmux you will also be able to keep your app running for an extended period of time after you disconnect from your box.
