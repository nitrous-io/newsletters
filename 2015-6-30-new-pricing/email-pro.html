We wanted to give you a heads up about an important pricing change we are making to all Nitrous Pro workspaces starting on July 1st.

Starting at midnight UTC on July 1st (5 pm PDT on June 30th), we will be changing our pricing for new workspaces as shown on our pricing page. Since you are an existing user, we will give you the month of July free.

How This Affects You

Since you are currently on the Pro plan, your monthly billing rate will remain the same rate of $64.99/month with the same container limit you had previously. We know that many of you were confused by the hourly billing and the minimum monthly fee, so we’ve decided to simplify with standard fixed prices.

As an existing user, your signup date will be July 1st for the purposes of the new plans. Since we’re giving you the entire month for free, your first paid bill will be on August 1st which will cover the entire month of August.

If you have more questions about the new pricing model, take a look at our pricing page. We are always working to provide the best development experience possible and have some great updates coming your way this summer so stay tuned!
