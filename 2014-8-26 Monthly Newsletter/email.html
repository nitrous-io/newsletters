The Future of Development

Here at Nitrous.IO we have some big ideas about what the future of development should look like. Just as other great startups revolutionized production environments, we want to do the same for your development environment.

This month David Dollar joined up with us to help make this vision a reality. David is the former head of the Developer Experience (DX) team at Heroku, has worked on many developer-facing tools, including Heroku's API, CLI, foreman, and the Heroku toolbelt.

Take a look at David's blog post about his vision for the future of development.
